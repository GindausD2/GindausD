# Max UI reference update — 17 October 2026

The supplied recordings show Persona's mobile website (15:38 recording) and Starcy's iPhone Home, Profile and Memory screens (16:01 recording). Max borrows the generous spacing, restrained typography, central orb, compact page navigation and rounded controls. Brand marks, product imagery, certification claims and Starcy's multicolour orb are not copied.

The native implementation uses a glacier-blue sphere with layered glass folds and a quiet halo, a cool white canvas, a Home/Profile capsule, a compact composer and two editable draft suggestions. Conversation history and photo attachment are in the composer plus menu. Compact and accessibility-size layouts prioritize the orb and composer, omit decorative introduction/suggestions, and consolidate secondary tools into More. Draft suggestions disappear once a draft has text, avoiding accidental replacement. Your day, speech playback and morning briefing remain reachable beneath it. Profile retains personal photos, names, Memory, Dynamic Island and Settings. Authentication now shares the cool palette; the Login/Signup slider and seven-day demo behavior are unchanged.

The orb and its caption play/stop Max. The microphone captures a voice-to-text draft for review before sending. Active states animate; the resting orb does not continuously redraw. Reduced motion, reduced transparency and older iOS glass fallbacks remain supported. Chat colours adapt to light/dark appearance. Screenshot fixtures include regular, compact, large text, dark, profile, conversation and voice draft states using isolated sample data. Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency remain system-controlled and need manual accessibility acceptance; capture fixtures do not override those read-only environment values.

Only interface code and capture fixtures changed. Physical-device microphone, haptic and Dynamic Island delivery still require device acceptance. Simulator captures do not establish those behaviors, real Apple sign-in, subscription purchases or live AI access.
